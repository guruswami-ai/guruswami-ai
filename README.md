### Paul Nevin · Guruswami

AI security researcher and adviser in Canberra, Australia. Twenty years in cyber investigation and national security, now building and testing agentic AI systems to learn where they help, where they fail, and how to defend them.

**How I work:** I build by directing AI agents, with every change reviewed, gated and logged. I publish negative results as readily as positive ones, because the method is the point.

#### Start here

- **[Flockinator](https://github.com/guruswami-ai/flockinator-public)**: a sixteen-node physical lab for studying emergent behaviour, trust and deception between small AI agents, with a bounded adversary node. Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026.
- **[mlx-benchmarks](https://github.com/guruswami-ai/mlx-benchmarks)**: 290 measured inference data points across 10 models on a five-node Apple Silicon cluster, with a cross-platform NVIDIA comparison.
- **[WCER](https://github.com/guruswami-ai/wcer)**: run a Mixture-of-Experts model on only the experts your workload uses, verified behaviour-identical across five models from four families.
- **[australian-english-llm](https://github.com/guruswami-ai/australian-english-llm)**: a falsifiable, stage-gated method for specialising a small LLM, including a published negative result.
- **[Genomic Weights Thesis](https://github.com/guruswami-ai/GenomicWeightsThesis)**: a refuted hypothesis, documented in full, and the adversarial review method that caught it.
- **[Not-Happy-Jan](https://github.com/guruswami-ai/not-happy-jan)**: multi-sensory feedback for AI coding agents. Hold music while your agent works, then a cheerful or furious local voice. Public alpha, built for fun.
